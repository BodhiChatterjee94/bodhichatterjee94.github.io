### 1. The Problem
A petrochemical client lacked centralized visibility into its key performance indicators and operational metrics[cite: 1]. Without interactive reporting, leadership struggled to track business performance consistently and spot opportunities for operational improvement[cite: 1].

### 2. Key Results
* **Revenue impact:** Raised invoice revenue by **15%**[cite: 1].
* **Operational visibility:** Enabled continuous KPI monitoring, business performance tracking, and targeted operational improvements[cite: 1].

### 3. Data
* **Scope:** Operational performance logs, billing/invoicing records, and business KPI datasets (kept general for client confidentiality)[cite: 1].
* **Preparation:** Consolidated operational and financial metrics into structured reporting tables for interactive querying.

### 4. Approach
I created interactive dashboards and analytical reports tailored to stakeholder KPIs, allowing business teams to drill down into operational performance and revenue drivers in real time[cite: 1].

### 5. Analysis
Surfacing operational bottlenecks alongside invoicing metrics helped stakeholders pinpoint where process improvements would have the most direct impact on revenue[cite: 1].

### 6. What I Learned
Dashboards drive measurable financial outcomes when they connect day-to-day operational KPIs directly to revenue metrics like invoicing, making the business impact immediately visible to decision-makers.
