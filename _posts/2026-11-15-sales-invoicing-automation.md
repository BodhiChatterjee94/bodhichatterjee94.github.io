### 1. The Problem
In a retail tyres and tubes operation, monitoring monthly sales performance and generating invoices relied heavily on manual spreadsheet work[cite: 1]. This repetitive process consumed significant weekly staff time and increased the risk of manual errors.

### 2. Key Results
* **Time saved:** Cut down at least **10 man-hours every week** of manual effort[cite: 1].
* **Process efficiency:** Streamlined invoice generation and standardized monthly sales performance tracking[cite: 1].

### 3. Data
* **Scope:** Monthly retail sales records, product SKUs, and customer invoicing data (kept anonymized for confidentiality)[cite: 1].
* **Preparation:** Structured raw sales inputs into clean, formula-driven tracking sheets to automate calculations and invoice outputs.

### 4. Approach
I designed an Excel-based invoicing and sales tracking solution that automated monthly performance summaries and streamlined the invoice generation workflow without requiring additional software licenses[cite: 1].

### 5. Analysis
Centralizing sales tracking and invoice generation into a single structured workbook eliminated duplicate data entry and gave the team an immediate view of monthly sales trends[cite: 1].

### 6. What I Learned
High-impact analytics doesn't always require complex infrastructure. A well-structured Excel solution tailored to a team's daily workflow can immediately reclaim hours of manual work every week[cite: 1].
