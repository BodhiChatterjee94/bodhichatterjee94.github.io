### 1. The Problem
Pharmacovigilance operations require strict accuracy and fast turnaround times when tracking drug safety and compliance metrics[cite: 7]. Manual data integration across multiple sources was creating reporting delays, data inconsistencies, and high weekly maintenance effort[cite: 7].

### 2. Key Results
* **Processing volume:** Improved claim process volumes by **10% MoM** through automated ETL pipelines and data validation[cite: 7].
* **Faster turnaround:** Reduced claim turnaround times by **6% YoY** via interactive Power BI reporting[cite: 7].
* **Time saved:** Eliminated at least **10 man-hours every week** through standardized data cleansing and quality assurance[cite: 7].

### 3. Data
* **Scope:** Multi-source drug safety records, operational claim logs, and compliance KPI datasets (details kept general to protect client confidentiality)[cite: 1, 7].
* **Preparation:** Built validation rules and cleansing transformations to ensure consistency, reliability, and accuracy across all downstream models[cite: 7].

### 4. Approach
I developed end-to-end ETL pipelines to automate data ingestion and validation, then built interactive Power BI dashboards so business stakeholders could monitor drug safety metrics and compliance KPIs without manual spreadsheet work[cite: 7].

### 5. Analysis
Centralizing operational and compliance metrics into a single Power BI view exposed bottlenecks in claim review stages and gave stakeholders immediate visibility into turnaround trends[cite: 7].

### 6. What I Learned
In regulated domains like pharmacovigilance, upstream data validation is just as important as the final dashboard. Automating quality checks early in the pipeline saved significant weekly effort and built stakeholder trust in the numbers[cite: 7].
