### 1. The Problem
A global CPG client needed to modernize how it extracted, transformed, and loaded data to support enterprise-wide analytics initiatives. Manual and fragmented data workflows were slowing down operational turnaround times and delaying downstream reporting[cite: 1].

### 2. Key Results
* **Faster operations:** Reduced operational turnaround time by **14% YoY**[cite: 1].
* **Scalable pipelines:** Streamlined end-to-end data extraction, transformation, and loading for enterprise analytics[cite: 1].

### 3. Data
* **Scope:** Enterprise operational and analytical datasets across global CPG business units (kept anonymized for client confidentiality)[cite: 1].
* **Preparation:** Automated extraction, transformation, and loading routines to standardize disparate source feeds[cite: 1].

### 4. Approach
I developed data engineering solutions and automated ETL processes to replace manual data handling, ensuring reliable, repeatable data delivery for enterprise analytics teams[cite: 1].

### 5. Analysis
Automating the core ingestion and transformation layers eliminated recurring bottlenecks in data refresh cycles, directly accelerating operational turnaround[cite: 1].

### 6. What I Learned
Building resilient ETL automation requires designing for data consistency upstream. Once ingestion and transformation are standardized, enterprise analytics teams can focus on insights rather than data prep.
